When deciding between ocean shipping and air freight, companies frequently weigh speed against budget. However, cargo density, shelf life, and holding costs are equally decisive factors.

### The Decision Matrix

| Dimension | Ocean Freight | Air Freight | Sea-Air Multimodal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transit Time** | 20–35 Days | 2–5 Days | 10–14 Days |
| **Relative Cost** | Baseline (1x) | 4x – 8x Baseline | 2x – 3x Baseline |
| **Ideal Cargo** | Heavy, bulk, high volume | High value, perishable, urgent | Seasonal retail, balanced stock |
